
## What is Delta?
- Delta is the amount an option price is expected to move based on a $1 change in the underlying stock
- Calls have positive delta, meaning they gain value when price goes up
- Puts have negative delta, meaning they gain value when price goes down

## Delta Ranges
- Deep in-the-money options: delta close to 1 (calls) or -1 (puts)
- ATM options: delta approximately 0.50
- OTM options: delta close to 0

## Key Concepts
- Delta tells you how much exposure to the underlying stock you have
- A delta of 0.50 means for every $1 the stock moves, the option moves $0.50
- As expiration approaches, the delta of in-the-money options moves closer to 1, and out-of-the-money options move closer to 0

## Delta as Probability
- Delta is also a rough approximation of the probability that an option will finish in-the-money
- A delta of 0.30 means roughly a 30% chance of finishing in-the-money
- A delta of 0.70 means roughly a 70% chance of finishing in-the-money
