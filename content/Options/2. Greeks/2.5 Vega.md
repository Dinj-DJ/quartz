
**## What is Vega?
- Vega measures the sensitivity of an option's price to changes in implied volatility
- Typically, as implied volatility increases, the value of options will increase — this becomes an increased range of potential movement for the stock
- Vega on a 30 day option on Stock XYZ with a $350 strike and a $350 price — Vega be $0.03
