

### Delta
- Measures how much an option's price changes for a $1 move in the underlying
- Approximates the probability the option finishes in-the-money
- Calls: positive delta (0 to 1). Puts: negative delta (0 to -1)
- ATM delta ≈ 0.50

### Gamma
- Measures the rate of change of delta
- Highest at ATM, decreases as you move away from the strike
- High gamma = delta changes quickly with price moves

### Theta
- Measures time decay — how much value an option loses per day
- Always works against the option buyer
- ATM options have the highest theta in dollar terms

### Vega
- Measures sensitivity to changes in implied volatility
- Long options benefit from rising volatility; short options benefit from falling volatility

### Rho
- Measures sensitivity to interest rate changes
- Less relevant for short-dated options; more relevant for longer-dated options
