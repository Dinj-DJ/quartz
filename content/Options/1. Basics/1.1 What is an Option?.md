
- Options are contracts that give the owner the right to buy or sell an asset at a fixed price (called the "strike price") for a specific period of time
- The seller of the option contract has the obligation to take the opposite side of the trade if and when the owner exercises the right to buy and sell the asset

## Anatomy of an Option: XYZ January 70 Call at $3.10

- **XYZ** — the stock the option is based on, usually represents 100 shares
- **January** — the month the option expires; last day to trade the option is usually the third Friday of that month
- **70** — the "strike price" for the stock; the stock will change hands at $70 if the option is exercised
- **Call** — the type of option; there are 2 types of options: "Calls" and "Puts"
- **$3.10** — that's the premium, or per share cost of the option
