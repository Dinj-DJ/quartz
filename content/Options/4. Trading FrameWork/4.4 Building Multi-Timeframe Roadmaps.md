## For Intraday Trading:
- You focus on higher timeframe zones and more frequent smaller timeframe updates (credits)
- Like the 15 min charts to identify next zones and more frequent smaller timeframe updates (credits)
- For Swing trading, you zoom out and use higher timeframes, use more frequent timeframe updates

## When Building a Swing Roadmap:
- Identify high zones and more frequent smaller timeframe updates
- -Confirm You using Fibonacci
- -Identify high concentrations zones
