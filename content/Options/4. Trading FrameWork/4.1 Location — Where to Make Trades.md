
## Location is One of the Most Important Concepts in Trading

- It answers the question: does this make sense from a risk-reward perspective?
- A good location is one where:
  - Does this make sense from a risk-reward perspective?
  - Your reward potential is clearly defined
  - Your Risk is clearly defined behind you

## Key Questions to Ask
- "Does this make sense from a risk-reward perspective?"
- "Your reward potential is clearly defined"
- "Is your Risk is clearly defined?"
- "Do you have strong support or resistance behind you?"
- "A good location is one where multiple risk-reward Perspective?"

## The Key Locations are Areas Where Multiple Factors Align:
- Buying near extremes, not in the middle
- Because there is no clear Price Balance instance

## These Assess All as Your "Validation" of Price Moves Detected
- Gamma levels
- ODTE levels
- Blue Stop
- Prior Reactions
- Beyond Near, your hurt is "invisible"
- If you don't have Potential, you don't have a trade

## Key Takeaway
- If you don't have Price moves detected, you don't have a trade
- Invisible signals mean you are trading blind
