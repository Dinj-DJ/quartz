
### How to use the DOM

- The DOM allows you to understand where Buyers and Sellers are in the market, 
- What we want to identify is where the is strength between Buyers and Sellers

Going from Left -- > Right on the Dom in the following image is Volume profile, Price (of Asset), Bid (Buy Limit), Market Sells, Market Buys, Ask (Sell limit), Bid and Ask for the day. 

![[DOM - Collumns.png|474]]


- The most important columns are the 2 middles one, The Mkt Buy, Mkt Sell column.

Remember - you can only fill the Bid when you get the Sell.
Remember - you can only fill the Ask when you get the Buy. 

- When we start to see the numbers on the Mkt Sell side start to get bigger and thicker, you will generally see downside pressure in the market, Remember- As a seller you are taking bids off, which will result in the market going down, (Vise Versa for other side). 

- As a general statement, When you have more buyers than sellers the market will go up, and vise versa. 


As we continue either up or down, at a certain point (in this example), buyers might get stuck as we continue up, and at a certain point the bid VP gets so thick that the buyers get stuck, then the other side takes control and we flip (And Vise Versa).
