
- If someone sells 3 Contracts (MKT), 3 Passive Buyers (limits) will fill that order
- If someone wants to buy 3 Contracts (MKT), 3 Passive Sellers (Limits) will fill that order.

## Logic

- Passive Sellers --> Fill Market Buyers
- Market Buyers --> Fill Passive sellers 

- Passive Buyers --> Fill Market Sellers
- Market Sellers --> Fill Market buyers

Bid - Limit Buy
@Bid - Agg Sellers

Ask - Limit Sell
@Ask - Agg Buyers


***It may sound confusing but just think if you want to Market order in (Agg Buy), the opposite side limit will need to fill you (Passive Sellers), and Vise Versa***


### ABS

- Abs is just Market sellers, selling in, A passive buyer fills all orders, absorbing them, and then aggressive market participants in the other way (Buy in this example.)

- Abs is normally over a few ticks, its continuous. 


## Icebergs 

- ![[IceBerg.png|176]] ![[IceBerg EG.png|169]]

- So this is what Icebergs will look like, imagine there are 10 passive buyers, we are currently trading down towards them (selling), once we get to that level like seen in the image, at the same level 10 Mkt Sell orders will get filled, and the 10 passive buyers will get wiped, that number will now be 0. 

- But then another 10 will get added to the same level (Icebergs will be the same number of cons), then they will get filled by Market sellers who will now be 20, and the passive buyers will get wiped, and this will continue to happen. 

***This will happen in milliseconds***
