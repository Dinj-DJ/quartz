
## What is Absorption 

- Absorption is when the market is trading at a key level, and there is continuous contracts flooding into the limit side and going in at market without actually moving the market. 


## What happens at Absorption 

- At a low on Abs, you will see multiple large bids that are resting. 
    Lets take 350 bids for example, when price trades through these, they will go through at market, so now there is 350 market sell orders. Now the expectation when we see market sell order sis to see price gradually drop because we are seeing more sellers coming into the market. 
- Then what happens is they add another 400 on the bid, these then get market filled and added to the market orders so now there is 750 market sell orders, and this is what absorption looks like. 
- The continues re add of limits that get filled at limits, and the market number continues to increase, but the market isn't dropping like it is expected to. 

- The Whole process of absorption is that you will get the reversal but in this case only when market buyers step in 

   ***Its one thing to get absorption but its another thing for the market buyers/sellers to really solidify that idea.*** 

  ***Abs can happen at multiple levels, remember it is continuous, its not only at one level.***
