- what your aim is with building a thesis is to eliminate noise, dot explains that he marks out his pivots, and support zones, and any pa in between above or below, is noise, he ignores it. 
- He explains that you need to know when you want to see what you want to see.
- You have to execute in places where it makes sense to see what you want to. 
- He explains that because the DOM is very discretionary sometimes you can make up signals that are not actually there

- with absorption you always want to see aggression or exhaustion from the other side. 

- Normally goes Abs -->Initiation -->Exh from other side and this initiates the entire move.
- You should only be reading the DOM inside of your pivots and that's it, this will eliminate a lot of noise, and can save u from a lot of losses.

- You always want to be combining HTF and LTF AMT. 

- Use delta to confirm your thesis![[Delta.png]] 
- In this situation we are bearish, and to confirm it we would want to see tons of buying delta being trapped in this area. 

- Sometimes u will see buying delta at the top of the move and they get absorbed, you will only see that they get absorbed after the other sides shows aggression 

- If buyers keep refreshing and we keep absorbing aggressive orders but then we also see no aggression from the other side (in this case sellers), and then we start pushing back  up, this is a dud signal- ![[Abs with not aggression.png]]
- If we see this it means the sellers have no intent behind them and buyers while exhaust sellers until we see aggression 

- ***Never enter off naked absorption always wait for one side to initiate.***
