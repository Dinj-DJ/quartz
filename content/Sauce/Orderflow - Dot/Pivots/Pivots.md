## Logic behind Pivots 


![[Logic - pivots.png]]

- So when we come into value, there is multiple levels we can play off. (The 2 Extremes, VAH/VAL, POC)
- The best level is one where institutions are active themselves 
   What this means is one where a lot of size is being moved

- Size as in whose moving the most size around - Who ever is doing that uses absorption as one of their tools to do that

---

- The logic behind why we cant break an abs level is because its a really dominant area for sellers/buyers 
![[Why Abs level dont break.png]] 
- As you can see in this example, if we are coming up to an abs level and aggressive buyers put 1000 contracts on, but then those 1000 contracts are met with passive sellers (limits) with another 1000 contracts, and then the sellers put another 500 cons on, and buyers only put 100 on because they are exhausted, the bid thins out andthen we dump in the other direction. 

### Break 

The opposite is also true 

- When you see us break above a pivot and accept into the higher prices, and re absorb the opposition (was buyers now sellers)
![[Breakout Pivot - Logic.png]]

- This causes a shift, now buyers are really strong and they are able to buy more - Now they are the ones adding 500 cons and sellers are now exhausted 

- This is why dot uses abs and exh in conjunction with each other

### How to confirm breakout 

![[Breakout Confirmation.png]]

- You can confirm a breakout by seeing how much volume was traded. 

- As you can see in the example, a breakout is confirmed by seeing that 500 cons were traded in that balance inside/below the pivot, and then in the breakout candles there was 1000 cons traded. This confirms the breakout by showing more volume was actually traded - andthen the fact that we created balance above shows they are accepting price in previous unfair value which confirms the breakout.
