(just miscellaneous notes, )

- when you are entering inside of value, your ignoring the auction. 

- Low volume directly tells u that price is not willing to stay in a certain area because one side is adding to many positions or is way to interested. 

- High volume shows u disinterest, that both sides are equally as interested.

- We want to get in on the 1 sided interest (extremes)

- Uncertainty itself is in name - your not certain that price is going to go here so u want to cover that area (where you SL should be)

- Value could be considered as somewhat certain because price has already been there, and it can go there again

- But it hasn't gone in the low volume area, because it hasn't gone there so you bet on the fact that it wont. 

**_this is the sort of logic dot uses.**

- A lot of the times dot puts his SL above Abs levels, because if the absorber stops absorbing the orders, the trade is invalid. 

- You need to get in at Low volume areas.
