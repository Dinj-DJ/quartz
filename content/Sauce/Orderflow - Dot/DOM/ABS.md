
There are 2 types of orders 

- Aggressive - These hit the bid or lift some offers, they actually go through to the exchange and get executed

- Passive - Limit orders, whose willing to transact at what price and how many cons. 

Don't complicate this

***Both of these need to be used in conjunction with each other*** 

- There are times when flow is very authentic, for Dot, Long gamma is very good (Long Regime).

- In short gamma we are a bit more volatile and flow is harder to read, you still can but don't trade open, or just be extremely picky with your entries. 

- When trading in the ranges you should be only trading at the extremes, never chase price in long gamma, can kind of get away with it in short gamma. 

#### ABS

- When a lot of aggressive orders trade into limits and the limits keep on re-loading. 

![[ABS on DOM.png]]
 - As we can see in the drawing, abs is shown by when there is 50 cons on the ask, and 50 buyers come in and lift the ask, but then the passive sellers re load those 50 cons and don't let them lift the ask, and when another 50 cons comes in, sellers re load again, which is Absorption. 

- Absorption doesn't only happen at one place, it happens at multiple ticks (a range)

- You need a location with Absorption, you want it to happen at extremes, then it needs to be followed by aggression. 

- if you see constant reloading and then aggression, that's your giveaway 

- Refreshing the DOM is extremely important - its a MUST

- We will usually give 1 or 2 re offers before making the actual move because absorption is continuous, and will continue to happen until the person absorbing has filled enough orders. 

- Dom is going to be most accurate at extremes and pivots - you don't need to read it all the time.

### Summary
- Aggressive orders trade into passive limits, and then they constantly keep slapping on more orders (refreshing)
