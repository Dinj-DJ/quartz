### AI summary 
**Bid = Passive Buyers** When someone posts a bid (a limit order to buy), they're sitting on the order book waiting for a seller to come to them. They're providing liquidity, not demanding it. They're passive because they don't initiate the trade — they wait.

**Bid = Aggressive Sellers** When a seller wants to exit _now_, they hit the bid — they cross the spread and fill against the resting buy order. That seller is the aggressive participant: they're taking liquidity and accepting the worse price to get immediate execution.

---


**Ask = Passive Sellers** When someone posts an ask (a limit order to sell), they're sitting on the order book waiting for a buyer to come to them. They're providing liquidity, not demanding it. They're passive because they don't initiate the trade — they wait.

**Ask = Aggressive Buyers** When a buyer wants to enter _now_, they lift the ask — they cross the spread and fill against the resting sell order. That buyer is the aggressive participant: they're taking liquidity and accepting the worse price to get immediate execution.
