**_Each SL should be different average size each time_**

##### SL
- The whole reason for a SL is to cover **uncertainty** (this is a non negotiable)
- if there isn't a lot of ABS at the low/high be skeptical about entering a trade in that direction - until
- SL- Below/Above failed auctions
- ABS is very good for protected lows/highs

### Two types of stop Losses

***you don't want to cover THE low, you want to cover Below/above the low.*** 
######  Long gamma 
1. Entering in low volume areas
- Covering low volume areas
- Don't chase price, long gamma is very mean reversion

2. In long gamma regime, can enter on any re - bid to the extreme until rotations complete. 
 **(this is only really if u miss the original entry @ the low volume extreme)**


- _logic behind this is that we cannot initiate trend without absorbing higher and higher prices (or vise versa), and without inventory moving higher and higher (or vise versa_)

**_if price has been somewhere - it can go there again_**
######  Short gamma 
- You can kind of chase price in short Gamma, dealers hedge with the trend which accelerates price
- In short gamma regime INT pivots are what Dot uses for re entry - must see proper aggression and proper break.

_link to probabilities - are we more likely to start rotation to the opposite side or breakout now and go lower/higher_

_**you need to remember you cant physically absorb all orders in one go, absorption is continuous over multiple price levels_**

## Summary 
- Stop loss should go below/above uncertainty, if there is absorption at the low/ high it becomes really strong

- Long gamma = never chase price, wait for low volume dip , confirm entry, place SL below/above real uncertainty where price should not go. Not at low, not at wick, uncertainty must be covered. 

- Short Gamma = Enter on any re bids with sufficient absorption, and SL can go below wicks, until rotation is complete.
