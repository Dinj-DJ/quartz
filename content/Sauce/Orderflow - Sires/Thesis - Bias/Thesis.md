
### What is Thesis/Bias?

- Thesis/Bias is just forming a conclusion on the market through various ways to expect where the markets will move and you trade based off that, usually derived from observing the balance of buying and selling activity, the aggressiveness of market participants, and the structure of orders so you would of course know when your bias will end or be wrong by certain factors that's when you re create your bias/thesis.
