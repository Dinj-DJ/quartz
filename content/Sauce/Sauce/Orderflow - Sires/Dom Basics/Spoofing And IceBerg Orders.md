

- An iceberg order is a large order broken into smaller visible chunks. Only a small portion is shown on the DOM, while the rest remains hidden that stack on that same level

### How to use it 

1. Watch for your level to hold with loads of orders hitting the bid/ask and stacking constantly 
2. Confirm that large volume is being held and is not being absorbed then wait for more buyers/sellers to add on within 2 ticks.
3. Then enter

### Spoofing Orders 

- Spoofing is when someone puts large visible orders to create a false impression of bid/ask limits, and then cancels them before they get filled. The intent is to move price or induce other traders to act on a fake signal. 


#### How to use it 

1. Watch for large visible orders that repeatedly appear and cancel when price approaches them 
2. Check whether volume is actually being traded into that level or if the visible size evaporates without meaningful prints .
3. If the level is likely spoofing, avoid entering with the perceived momentum and instead wait for genuine absorption or for the fake orders to be removed, then enter with confirmation from real prints and sustained liquidity.
