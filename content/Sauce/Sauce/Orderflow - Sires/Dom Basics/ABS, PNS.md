### Pulling and Stacking 
 ![[Unfinished Auction.png|360]]

- This is what an unfinished auction looks like (numbers are not relevant), if the Dom has a flat top and bottom like that it is considered an unfinished auction. 
- Price usually will sweep These areas and then continue in the opposite direction. 
![[Finished Auction.png|345]]

- As seen here at the top there is what's considered a finished auction, its when there is one number on top, can be on either side (left or right) and (bottom or top)
- At the bottom we can see an unfinished auction, So what will we do? We will most likely sweep the unfinished auction and then continue in the opposite direction (up in this case)![[UFN - FA Eg.png|435]]

- So as seen here, price sweeps the UFA, makes it an FA and then continues up, price will do whatever it wants after the sweep it doesn't always need to continue in the opposite direction. 


1. **Stacking** = traders keep adding _limit buy orders_ on the bid or _limit sell orders_ on the ask (ask is also called the offer).
    - If bids are stacking and **bid delta** is rising → looks like strong buy support.
    - If asks are stacking and **ask delta** is rising → looks like strong sell pressure.
2. **Pulling** = traders remove those stacked limit orders before price trades there.
    - If bids pull while **bid delta** is positive → buyers lose support, price can drop.
    - If asks pull while **ask delta** is negative → sellers lose pressure, price can rise.

In short: **stacking shows intent, pulling shows fluff. Delta confirms who’s actually aggressive (buyers or sellers). This is how you confirm the pulling and stacking**


***You want Absorption after a Finished Auction has happened***
