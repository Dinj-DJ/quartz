## Advanced 

![[Gamma Tps.png|697]]This isn't advanced i just didn't know what to name it. 


- As you can see in the graph it measures Daily returns from 2015-2016 based on what regime gamma is in (Long or Short).
- From the graph provided, we can see that daily expansions and declines are clearly accelerated when in short gamma (left side) - This is due to Short gamma accelerating price and Long gamma suppressing it. 
- And then in long gamma we can see that the daily expansions tighten, we get the (Fan Like Pattern). 
- Basically what this means that when we are in a long gamma regime (suppression), we should be making our TP's shorter, And when in Short gamma regime (Acceleration) we should be extending them to take full advantage of heightened and suppressed daily ranges
