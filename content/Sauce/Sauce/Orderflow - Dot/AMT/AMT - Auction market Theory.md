![[balance.png|279]]
- Balance - Neither sellers nor buyers are in control
- When we see this happen we will be in a tight range. buyers and sellers will step in at VAL, VAH.
#### Reason this happens
- The market itself doesn't have enough info to establish a trend 
- Or reached a point where neither buyers nor sellers are interested in putting long term positions, and to initiate a trend. 

#### When we see breakouts happen
- When markets have a bit more information
- Usually happens at set times (macro news, (9:30mo)

#### When we are in balance 
- Only two things we can do is either move out of balance, or stay within
- Both, the model should be able to solve/catch both scenarios 
###### Fades 
- Move out of balance, and then come back in 
![[Responsive.png|91]]

###### Initiations 
- One side takes control
 ![[Initiate.png|96]]

### How you can play of these extremes
- The upper part is VAH (which is unfair for buyers) - Once we come into Low volume area which is just above, you can expect to see sellers take initiative

- Vise versa for buyers - When we come into VAL (unfair for sellers) - Buyers take initiative. 

_**The frame work for the model relies on what prices are fair for who (buyers or sellers)**

### What can price do 
- if we are in a balance, price can do a few things
