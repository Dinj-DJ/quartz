#### Tools we use to confirm

- Absorption - One side is very interested, but the other side come in and absorb all the orders, and then aggression will show

- Exhaustion - One side is just completely not interested (Can see on DOM)


### Proper breakout 
1. Coming into extreme 
2. Break above
3. Shift value
4. Fail to auction within extreme 
5. Re bid or re offer
6. Then evaluate if we are absorbing
7. Trade to opposite side for a complete rotation
